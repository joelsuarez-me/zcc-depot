---
name: Portable section briefs
description: Why the site offers individual topic briefs and how future content should respect that purpose.
---

Educational sections should be independently usable as concise Print brief / Download PDF takeaways, using the same executive visual language as agenda and research exports.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested extending the comparison brief across appropriate sections so people new to the site can download each individual topic.

**How to apply:** Keep this option available when adding substantial learning sections or individual industry use cases. Preserve the topic context, relevant qualifications and source references. Make clear when a simulation brief reflects only its current view rather than a complete replay. Keep specialized agenda/research exports and their save/access rules; never repurpose private form values, hidden panels or presenter notes into generic takeaways.

Brief PDFs should include illustrations aligned with the subject, larger website-style sans-serif typography, and polished executive formatting for lists and information. Do not restore a “Continue exploring” section.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested these changes because the earlier brief typography, bullets and layout looked too plain.

**How to apply:** Apply the visual treatment consistently to individual topic briefs and the full comparison brief. Keep necessary source attribution compact, without recreating a large exploration section. Concept illustrations must be labeled and must not imply measured results, enabled features or an actual customer deployment.
